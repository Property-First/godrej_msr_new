Welcome To GODREJ MSR CITY PHASE 2

GODREJ MSR CITY PHASE 2 is an upcoming residential development by Godrej Properties in Shettigere, North Bangalore. Spread across approximately 54 acres as part of a larger integrated township, Phase 2 offers 805+ residences across 3 high-rise towers with spacious 2 & 3 BHK apartments.

The towers are planned with a 2B + Ground + 15 floors configuration and are part of a larger township planned with approximately 20 acres of green spaces and around 70% open space. Residents will also have access to a large approximately 1.5 lakh sq. ft. clubhouse along with a range of lifestyle, fitness, sports and recreational amenities.

The project features amenities including swimming pools, a fitness centre, gym, badminton court, sports courts, jogging track, children's play area, amphitheatre, multipurpose spaces and landscaped open areas, creating a well-planned environment for families and residents.

Strategically located at Shettigere, North Bangalore, the project offers convenient access to NH 44, Doddajala Metro Station, Kempegowda International Airport, Prestige Tech Cloud, KIADB Hardware & Aerospace Park and other key destinations across North Bangalore.

The 2 BHK residences are available in approximately 1,200 - 1,300 sq. ft. and start from ₹1.59 Cr*. The 3 BHK residences are available in approximately 1,600 - 1,730 sq. ft. and 1,900 - 2,200 sq. ft. configurations, with pricing available on request.
